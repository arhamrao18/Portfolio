Problem It Solves

Typing out every question isn't always convenient. This assistant lets you just talk — ask anything, get a spoken answer back, and if there's more than one way to solve the problem, it gives you three options to pick from instead of guessing for you.

Key Features
  • Personal AI voice assistant
  • Answers open-ended questions
